How To Play

A five-letter word is hidden. Spell your guess in the brass window across the middle of the board, then submit it.

Slide a column up or down, or slide a row left or right. Everything wraps around, and the spare slot at each end of the window is what you slide letters through.

A column only holds the letters it happens to hold, so sliding rows is how you fetch a letter it does not have.

Submitting marks your guess the way Wordle does: green for the right letter in the right place, amber for a letter in the word but somewhere else, grey for a letter that is not in it at all.

You get six guesses. Sliding is free — only submitting costs you one.
