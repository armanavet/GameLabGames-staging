How To Play

Three words are hidden in one cube. Every square carries a letter, and you turn the faces until a word lines up on the numbered squares of the front face.

You are never told the words. The squares tell you how close you are, the way Wordle does:

Green — the right letter in the right square.
Amber — that letter is in the word, but belongs in a different square.
Grey — that letter is not in the word at all.

The numbers show the order: the four corners first, then the centre, then an edge. So a four-letter word sits on the corners alone.

All three words share the same cube, so switching between them changes nothing — and every turn gives you clues about all three at once.

Use the turn buttons, or the keys T / B / L / R / F / K for top, bottom, left, right, front and back. Hold Shift to turn the other way. Drag the cube to look around it, and Reset view to face the front again.

No word is more than six turns from where you start. Your score is the turns you take and the time on the clock.
