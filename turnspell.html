How To Play

Every square on the cube carries a letter. Turn the faces until the goal word appears on the front face, reading left to right, top to bottom.

The tinted squares are the ones the word has to fill — they are numbered in reading order. A square turns green when it holds the right letter.

Use the turn buttons, or the keys T / B / L / R / F / K for top, bottom, left, right, front and back. Hold Shift to turn the other way. Drag the cube to look around it.

There are three goal words each day, and none of them is ever more than a few turns away. Your score is the number of turns you take and the time on the clock.
